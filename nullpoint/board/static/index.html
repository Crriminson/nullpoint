Using Nullpoint

With your eyes

  • Look left or right past the card to move the highlight. Keep looking to keep moving (up to 4 steps), look back at the card to stop.
  • Blink firmly to say the highlighted phrase. Normal blinks do nothing.
  • Close your eyes for 2 seconds, then open them to call for help. A soft tone at 2 s means "open now". Longer closures are rest and never call anyone.

Keys

  • ← → move · Space select · E call for help · C clear the alarm · R say it again
  • K 10-second setup · N find your null zone · T phrase test
  • S simulated nystagmus (off, 5°, 10°, 15°) · A adaptation on/off · D show how the gaze is read
